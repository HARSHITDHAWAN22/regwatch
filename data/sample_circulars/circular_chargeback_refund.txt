Turn Around Time for Refund of Failed Transactions

1. In case of a failed transaction where the beneficiary account is not credited but the remitter's account has been debited, the amount shall be reversed (auto-reversal) to the remitter within a maximum of T+1 working day (T being the date of the failed transaction).

2. Where the auto-reversal under paragraph 1 does not occur within the prescribed period, the Regulated Entity shall pay a compensation of Rs. 100 per day of delay to the affected customer, credited automatically without requiring a separate claim from the customer.

3. For chargebacks raised by a customer for goods/services not received or not as described, the acquiring bank/PA shall provide a provisional credit to the customer's account within 7 working days from the date of the chargeback claim, pending resolution with the merchant.

4. Merchants shall be given 10 working days to respond to a chargeback with supporting evidence, failing which the chargeback shall be deemed to be accepted and settled in favour of the customer.

5. This circular applies uniformly across all payment rails, including UPI, cards, IMPS, and net banking, unless a rail-specific NPCI circular provides for a shorter timeline, in which case the shorter timeline shall prevail.

6. This circular is not applicable to disputes involving cross-border remittances, which continue to be governed by FEMA regulations and correspondent banking agreements.

7. This circular takes effect from January 1, 2027.
