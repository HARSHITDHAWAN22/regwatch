Why RegWatch Matters: Real-World Regulatory Non-Compliance Penalties

Companies in India's financial sector get fined for regulatory non-compliance all the time. It's not rare, and it's not cheap. The cases below are real and sourced, and they cover several regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI) and industries.
Most of them aren't fraud - they're cases where a company simply didn't track, understand, or act on a regulatory circular in time. That's the exact gap RegWatch is trying to close.

Note: These examples show the kind of risk RegWatch is built to address. They're not claims that RegWatch would have stopped any specific penalty below - the real causes (escrow monitoring gaps, internal audit failures, etc.) are usually bigger than just "didn't read the circular."


UPI / Digital Payments (PPI Issuers)

PhonePe - fined more than once for the same kind of issue

Rs 21 lakh (2025): PhonePe's escrow account balance dropped below the value of outstanding prepaid payment instruments (PPIs) and pending merchant payments on certain days. It also didn't report the shortfall to RBI right away, which it was required to do. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/rbi-fines-phonepe-21-lakh-for-escrow-shortfall-ppi-norms-violation-125091201312_1.html
Rs 1.39 crore (2020): Fined again for PPI regulatory norm violations.
Rs 1 crore (2019): Its first penalty in this category, for non-compliance with PPI guidelines. Source: https://www.medianama.com/2025/09/223-rbi-fines-phonepe-21-lakh/

Joint RBI action - six entities, Rs 5.8 crore total (2020) PNB, Sodexo, Muthoot Vehicle & Asset Finance, QwikCilver Solutions, PhonePe, and Delhi Metro Rail Corporation were all fined together for not following PPI-related regulatory guidelines under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/economy-policy/rbi-levies-over-rs-5-8-cr-fine-on-6-entities-including-pnb-sodexo-phonepe-120112001364_1.html

Paytm Payments Bank - Rs 5.39 crore (2023) This one covered several different circular categories at once: KYC norms, maximum end-of-day balance limits, cyber security incident reporting, and rules on securing mobile banking/UPI apps. It later led to a ban on adding new customers. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/companies/news/rbi-slaps-rs-5-39-crore-penalty-on-paytm-pb-for-violating-series-of-norms-123101201150_1.html

Paytm (One97 Communications) and its CEO - GST penalty (2025) Rs 1.19 crore on the company and Rs 59.94 lakh on CEO Vijay Shekhar Sharma personally, over alleged non-compliance in issuing tax invoices. A good reminder that regulatory risk isn't just an RBI thing - tax authorities count too. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/people/paytm-its-ceo-fined-with-gst-penalty-over-alleged-non-compliance-125020401714_1.html


Banks

State Bank of India (SBI) - Rs 7 crore (2019) One of the biggest penalties handed to any Indian bank at the time. It covered income recognition and asset classification (IRAC) norms, current account rules, CRILC large-credit reporting, and fraud reporting requirements. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/rbi-slaps-rs-7-crore-penalty-on-sbi-for-violating-various-regulatory-norms-119071600043_1.html

SBI - Rs 1.30 crore (2023, separate case) Fined again, this time for not complying with rules on 'Loans and Advances - Statutory and Other Restrictions' and intra-group transaction guidelines. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-imposes-monetary-penalty-on-sbi-punjab-sind-bank-and-indian-bank-123092501054_1.html

Indian Overseas Bank - Rs 2.2 crore (2023) Part of this fine came from failing to roll out ATM security controls (end-to-end encryption between the ATM terminal and switch) within RBI's deadline - a straightforward case of missing a compliance date. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/rbi-imposes-rs-2-2-crore-penalty-on-indian-overseas-bank-for-non-compliance-123060200972_1.html

Deutsche Bank AG - Rs 50 lakh (2025) Fined for not complying with RBI's rules on reporting large credit exposures to the Central Repository of Information on Large Credits (CRILC). Source: https://newsonair.gov.in/rbi-fines-deutsche-bank-%E2%82%B950-lakh-yes-bank-%E2%82%B929-6-lakh-for-regulatory-violations/

Fino Payments Bank - Rs 29.6 lakh (2025) Fined for not following payments bank operational rules. This isn't new for them either - they were fined Rs 1 crore back in 2018 for opening accounts despite RBI explicitly telling them to stop. Source: https://www.medianama.com/2025/06/223-rbi-fino-rs-29-60-lakh-payments-bank-rules/

Equitas Small Finance Bank - Rs 65 lakh (2025) Fined over foreclosure charge and pre-payment penalty rules, plus collateral-free agricultural loan norms.

India Post Payments Bank - Rs 26.70 lakh (2025) Fined for not following 'Customer Service in Banks' directions. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/companies/news/rbi-penalises-equitas-small-finance-bank-india-post-payments-bank-125013101573_1.html

14 banks fined at once (2021) All fined for non-compliance with directions on lending to NBFCs and related bank-finance guidelines. Worth noting - compliance gaps like this often happen across the whole industry at once, not just to one unlucky company. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=51863
