Additional Factor of Authentication for Digital Payment Transactions

1. In terms of extant guidelines, all digital payment transactions, whether domestic or cross-border, shall be authenticated by an Additional Factor of Authentication (AFA), except where specifically exempted.

2. It has been decided to revise the threshold for mandatory AFA on card-not-present recurring transactions from Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 25,000 per transaction, with effect from the date of this circular.

3. For fund transfers initiated through internet banking or mobile banking applications exceeding Rs. 2,00,000 in a single transaction, an additional confirmation step involving a cooling-off period of 4 hours before the first such transfer to a new beneficiary shall be implemented, in addition to existing AFA requirements.

4. This cooling-off period shall not apply to transfers to beneficiaries already registered and used for at least one successful transaction in the preceding 30 days.

5. Regulated Entities shall ensure that the AFA mechanism is not solely reliant on SMS-based OTP where the customer has opted for app-based authentication, in order to reduce SIM-swap-related fraud risk.

6. This circular does not alter the existing exemption for UPI Lite offline transactions or small-value contactless transactions, which continue to be governed by their respective specific circulars.

7. Entities shall implement the changes under paragraph 3 within 90 days of this circular.

Yours faithfully,

(Chief General Manager)
